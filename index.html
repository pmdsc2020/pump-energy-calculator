TDH = Static + Friction + Pressure
P (kW) = Q × TDH × Derating ÷ (C × Ep)
C = 360 for m³/h · 102 for l/s
Hours = Volume ÷ Q
Energy = Hours × Motor kW ÷ Em
HP = kW × 1.34
